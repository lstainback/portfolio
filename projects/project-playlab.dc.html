Play Lab Bot

Using an AI interface to make learning feel less like a course and more like a conversation.

Play Lab is an AI-powered website designed to explore what learning can look like when the interface itself becomes the teacher. This project is an experiment in curiosity-driven design, where the goal is engagement first.

Traditional eLearning asks learners to follow a path. Play Lab asks them to explore one. The bot interface removes the structure of a course and replaces it with conversation, meeting learners in a format they already know how to use.

This project explores what happens when you stop thinking about learning as a thing that happens to people and start designing it as something they choose to do.
