Claude Skills for Learning

An evaluation tool built to help employees track their impact and walk into review season ready.

Building with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, means thinking like an instructional designer and a prompt engineer at once. This project uses Claude to guide employees through a structured self-reflection experience, showing how AI can power tools that feel personal, not robotic.

The skill walks users through capturing their wins, impact, and growth in a format that's ready for performance reviews and career conversations. It's designed to lower the barrier to self-advocacy making it easier for people to articulate value they often underestimate.

This project lives at the intersection of instructional design and AI tool development: every prompt is a design decision, and every response a learning moment.
