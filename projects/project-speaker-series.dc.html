Speaker Series: Intra-Company Knowledge Share

An end-to-end learning program, built from needs analysis to evaluation strategy.

This project plan documents a full-cycle learning initiative I designed during my internship at SentinelOne. The Speaker Series addresses a real organizational need: breaking down silos and connecting teams across the company. Rather than adding another meeting to the calendar, I adapted the program to fit how people actually work, building an asynchronous podcast, newsletter, and chat channel that support learning on their own terms.

This project demonstrates my ability to move through a full ADDIE cycle, actively integrate AI into the design process, and build solutions that start with listening to what learners actually need.
