Articulate 360

Two different modules built in Articulate 360. 

Articulate 360 is the industry standard for eLearning development, known for its ability to build everything from simple knowledge checks to complex branching scenarios. Here you'll find two modules that show my range: one technical, one conceptual, both designed with real learners in mind.

Good instructional design isn't one-size-fits-all. These modules demonstrate how the same tool can serve very different learning goals, and the design decisions behind each one are intentional.

Technical Module

Adding Data to a Shared Google Sheet

Use this module to learn the technical aspects of adding your data to a large multi-tab Google Sheet. Learn the ins and outs of contributing your data without breaking the sheet.
