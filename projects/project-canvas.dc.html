Canvas LMS

Building real learning experiences inside the LMS that runs higher ed and corporate training alike.

Canvas is one of the most widely used learning management systems in both education and corporate settings. This work demonstrates how I structure content, organize learner pathways, and build modules that feel intuitive from the inside out.

Good LMS design is invisible, when it works learners don't think about navigation, they think about learning. That's the standard I hold myself to: every module organized so the learner always knows where they are, what's next, and why it matters.

This project covers course architecture, page design, and the intentional choices behind how content is sequenced and surfaced.
