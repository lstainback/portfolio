Lauren Stainback | Instructional Designer

Instructional designer specializing in AI-powered corporate learning that puts people first

Hi, I'm Lauren!

I'm an instructional designer who believes the best learning experiences live at the intersection of smart design and genuine human connection. I use AI to build corporate learning that's sharper, faster, and still deeply rooted in how people actually grow.

My path here wasn't straight, and I think that's what makes my work different. I spent years in outdoor education leading corporate team building experiences and facilitating leadership development programs. I designed activities, read groups in real time, and learned how to create the conditions where real learning happens. Later I found myself in fine dining, earning my sommelier certification and teaching wine education to restaurant staff. It turns out that helping someone understand terroir and helping someone build a new skill aren't that different. Both require you to meet people where they are.

Now I bring that facilitation instinct into corporate L&D, paired with AI tools that make the design process smarter and faster. I'm completing my Instructional Design certificate from Mesa College this November, and my internship at SentinelOne gave me hands-on experience building real learning solutions inside a growing organization.

I'm an instructional designer who thinks the best corporate learning starts with understanding people, not just content. AI helps me get there more efficiently. The rest is just paying attention to people.
